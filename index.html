FAQ

Questions students ask first.

Is this real money?

No. Pay Me Now is a prototype built for coursework. Every name, job and balance is mock data and nothing moves any real money.

Does early pay cost anything?

No. There is no interest and no fee. You can take up to 50% of what you have earned, and the rest arrives on payday.

How is early pay funded?

In this concept the university advances it from a float, and recovers it from approved pay on payday. The department console shows how much of the float is in use.

What if I forget to tap?

After the shift ends you can ask for a manual sign-off. Staff see it flagged as "No tap" and decide whether to approve it.

Does NFC work on my phone?

On Chrome for Android over HTTPS you can turn on phone NFC under Card controls and hold a real tag to your phone. Everywhere else, taps are simulated so you can still try every step.
